A vetted mentorship marketplace for the MENA region — weekly 1-on-1 sessions with operators a few steps ahead. Built around a track-based matching flow: mentees submit a brief, the team hand-picks three vetted mentors within 24 hours, and a 20-minute fit call precedes any commitment. Sessions run as 60-minute LiveKit video calls with async messaging in between, plus a Teams plan for per-seat budgets, admin dashboards, and SSO.
