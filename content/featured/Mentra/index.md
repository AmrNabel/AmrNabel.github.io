A MENA-focused mentorship marketplace pairing paid one-to-one voice and video sessions with vetted human mentors and an on-demand Arabic-speaking AI voice mentor. Sessions run in Arabic or English and are paid in local currency, with recordings, written feedback after every call, and mentor payouts built in. Live in Egypt and Saudi Arabia.
